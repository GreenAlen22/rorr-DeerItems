# Changelog

All notable DeerItems changes.

## [3.0.15]

### Added

- `deer_give` console command: gives you any DeerItems item or equipment (host or single player); a player number as the third argument gives it to another player. Type it without arguments to list the ids.
- `deer_stats` console command: prints your stats and skill cooldowns.
- `deer_beast` console command: summons Ingrown Idol's beast next to you (host or single player).

### Changed

- DeerItems no longer requires RoRR Modding Toolkit.
- Ingrown Idol's beast is redrawn to match the game's art: a gaunt wendigo of roots and moss with a deer skull for a head.
- English names: Bounty is now Bounty Tag, Defocused Prism is now Unfocus Prisma, Sporebloom is now Spore O Bloom.
- Faceted Jade, Obereg, Maslenitsa, Shield Generator, Deepcore GK2 and Cheat Death now drop from Utility chests instead of Healing chests, and Ice Mixture instead of Damage chests, matching the game's own armor, shield and slowing items.

### Fixed

- Magic Missile plays its own hit and launch sounds instead of AtG Missile Mk. 0's.
- Elephant Gun's bonus critical hits on bosses now show as critical hits.
- With Artifact of Command, Charlatan's Die and the Standing Requisition Chest give a Command crate of the item's rarity instead of the item.
- The Standing Requisition Chest no longer appears in later runs with the same choices as your first one, even without the item.
- Ingrown Idol's indicator no longer jumps to the left of the screen when you take damage.
- Enemies taunted by Ingrown Idol's beast no longer throw errors when they path toward it across platforms.
- Shine, O Sun's area is now a circle instead of a tall oval.
- Ravenous Vine's ring sits slightly lower.
- Multiplayer: Hunger and Soul Jar no longer leave a second black hole or an endless invisible clone on other players' screens.
- Multiplayer: Bloody Dividends, Bounty Tag and Bounty Contract now pay gold to clients who hold them.
- Gold from Bloody Dividends, Bounty Tag, Bounty Contract, Gold Bar and Cashback Chip now counts in the run report's total gold.
- Multiplayer: Ingrown Idol's indicator now fills up for clients.
- Totemetry's attack speed bonus now starts when the totem appears and ends on time, and works for clients.
- Multiplayer: Concentration's ready marker on clients now goes out after the empowered hit.

## [3.0.14]

### Added

- **66 available items:** 24 white, 21 green, 16 red, and 5 equipment items.
- 9 new white items: Bloody Dividends, Bounty Tag, Cheat Death, Concentration, Elephant Gun, Faceted Jade, Heavy Lungs, and Tankbuster.
- 11 new green items: Assembly Blueprint, Bounty Contract, Cashback Chip, Charlatan's Die, Contagion Barb, Golden Sneakers, Laughing Fungus, Loot Fever, Magic Missile, Reserve Capacitor, and Standing Requisition.
- 12 new red items: Army Surplus, Cascade Failure, Dombra, Ingrown Idol, Leacked Capacitor, Mosquito Net, Orbital Lens, Ravenous Vine, Rift Beacon, Rust Mite, Twisted Opinion, and Violet Frenzy.
- 5 equipment items: Annihilator, Hunger, One Army Man, Soul Jar, and Tactical Visor.
- The Standing Requisition Chest interactable.
- The Cernunnos actor.
- Two artifacts: Artifact of Droneman and Artifact of Technician.
- Mod configuration: toggle each item's loot availability individually or manage all items at once.

### Changed

- Moved "Green Tea" from the white to the green tier. And "Unfocus Prisma" from the green to the white tier
- Reworked and rebalanced existing white, green, and red items, including their effects, descriptions, visual assets, and sounds.
- Expanded Russian and English localization.
- Improved content loading and added ReturnsAPI support for multiplayer.
